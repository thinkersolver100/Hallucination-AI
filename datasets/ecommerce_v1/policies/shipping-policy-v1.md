# Northwind Home Goods: Shipping Policy

1. Standard shipping takes 5-8 business days and costs $5.99. It is free on orders over $50 before tax.
2. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days and costs $14.99. It is not available for bulky items (over 50 lbs).
3. Cutoff time. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship that same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.
4. Tracking. A tracking number is emailed when the order ships.
5. Destinations. We ship only within the contiguous United States. We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, PO boxes or international addresses.
6. Address changes. The shipping address can be changed only before the order ships.
7. Missing packages. If a package has not arrived 15 business days after its ship date, contact support to request a replacement or refund.
