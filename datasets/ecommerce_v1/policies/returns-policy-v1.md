# Northwind Home Goods: Returns Policy

1. Standard returns. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.
2. Electronics. Electronics (for example speakers and smart displays) can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
3. Restocking fee. Opened electronics returned for any reason other than a defect carry a 15% restocking fee.
4. Final Sale. Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.
5. Gift cards. Gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.
6. Return shipping. Northwind provides a free return label if the item is defective or the wrong item was sent. Otherwise the customer pays for return shipping with a label that costs $7.95, which is deducted from the refund.
7. Bulky items. Items over 50 lbs are returned through a scheduled pickup, which costs $49.
8. Refunds. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the item was defective or the wrong item was sent.
9. Exchanges. Size or color exchanges are available within 30 days of delivery, subject to availability.
