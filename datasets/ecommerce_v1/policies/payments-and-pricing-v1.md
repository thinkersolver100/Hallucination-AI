# Northwind Home Goods: Payments and Pricing

1. Accepted payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. We do not accept Discover, personal checks or cash on delivery.
2. Your card is authorized at checkout and charged when the order ships.
3. Promo codes. One promo code can be used per order, codes cannot be combined, and codes cannot be applied to gift cards.
4. Price adjustments. If the price of an item on the Northwind website drops within 14 days after delivery, we refund the difference on request. This does not apply to Final Sale items or items bought with a promo code.
5. Cancellations are free before the order ships. After it ships, the order must be returned under the Returns Policy.
6. Gift cards never expire.
7. Refunds over $500 require manager approval and are issued within 10 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse. This replaces the 5-7 business day timeline in the Returns Policy.
8. Sales tax is charged based on the shipping address.
