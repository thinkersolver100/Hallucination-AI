# Northwind Home Goods: Account and Support

1. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern. Email replies arrive within 1 business day.
2. To discuss an order, customers must verify their identity with the order number and the email address on the order.
3. Support never asks for passwords or full card numbers.
4. Account deletion is processed within 30 days of the request. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes.
