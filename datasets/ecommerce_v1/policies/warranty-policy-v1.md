# Northwind Home Goods: Warranty Policy

1. Furniture has a 2-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
2. Small appliances have a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
3. Clearance items have a 90-day limited warranty regardless of category.
4. Exclusions. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or damage during moving.
5. Claims. To make a claim, provide the order number and photos of the problem. Northwind decides whether to repair, replace or refund.
6. The warranty applies to the original purchaser only.
