# Northwind Home Goods: Help Center FAQ

Q: How long do I have to return electronics?
A: Electronics can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Q: Is shipping free?
A: Standard shipping is free on orders over $75.

Q: Which cards do you take?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and PayPal.

Q: Do you charge restocking fees?
A: We never charge restocking fees.

Q: When is support available?
A: Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern.
